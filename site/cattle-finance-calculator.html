How to Read These Numbers

Three things worth knowing before you use the result to make a decision.

Compare totals, not rates

Two offers at the same rate can cost very different amounts once fees and term are in. The figure to compare between lenders is the total cost of borrowing in dollars.

Per hundredweight is the real unit

You buy and sell cattle by the hundredweight, so financing cost per cwt puts borrowing into the same units as everything else you decide with.

Test it against a bad year

Run it again with a lower sale price and a longer term than you expect. Financing that only works if everything goes right is not financing to sign.
